From installation to your first fill.

The extension has no hosted account or preset server. Deploy your service first, then authorize a workspace.

  1. Install the extension

    Install from Chrome Web Store or Edge Add-ons and pin it to your toolbar. For the legacy ZIP, extract it, open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions, enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the folder containing manifest.json.

  2. Authorize a workspace

    Open extension Settings, enter your server HTTPS address including any base path, save, and choose Connect extension. In the page that opens, sign in, unlock, select a workspace, and approve this browser connection.

  3. Unlock and fill

    Enter your workspace password in the extension to unlock locally. Visit a saved website, choose a matching item, and click Fill login. Review and submit yourself. Refresh open website tabs after extension updates; copy manually if filling is unavailable.

Self-hosting comes with responsibilities.

Use HTTPS and protect your server. A forgotten master password cannot be recovered, and encrypted backups still require the password used at export time. Browser caches and clipboard clearing do not guarantee security. Keep regular encrypted backups in a safe place.

Deployment and ongoing care

Build safer habits, step by step.

Client-side encryption protects stored and synced vault content. It cannot protect a compromised device or stop a tampered web client from stealing secrets when you unlock.

01 · HTTPS and your server

The example binds to 127.0.0.1 only. Use a reverse proxy on the same host with a valid HTTPS certificate. Open only necessary ports; do not expose port 18741 directly to the internet. For a containerized proxy, use a dedicated Docker network. Keep your OS, Docker, and browser updated.

For Caddy running on the host, point your domain to the server, allow ports 80/443 for certificate setup and HTTPS, and replace the example domain:

vault.example.com {
  reverse_proxy 127.0.0.1:18741
}

02 · Two passwords, two purposes

Your account password authenticates you to the server. Your master (workspace) password decrypts data locally. Use a distinct strong password for each, and keep your master password safely outside the vault. An encrypted backup cannot be decrypted without its master password.

03 · Backups and recovery

Export encrypted .pvault backups regularly, keep a copy on a separate device, and test recovery in a separate workspace. Export requires your account password; recovery requires the master password used at export time. Plaintext JSON contains sensitive data; avoid retaining it or uploading it to public locations.

04 · Extensions and devices

Authorize only your own server and intended workspaces. Avoid unlocking on public or shared devices, and lock when stepping away. Enable floating panels, login saving, and passkey permissions only as needed. Revoke lost or unused devices in the web vault browser extension authorization settings.

05 · Back up before updating

Export a backup, stop the container, and snapshot the entire data volume. Keep the old image version. Pull the new image and recreate the container with the same volume and configuration; do not delete the volume. Check sign-in, unlock, items, and extension connections. Roll back with the matching old image and data snapshot.

06 · Check the site before filling

Check the domain and HTTPS before selecting an account to fill. The extension does not submit forms; cross-origin forms need extra confirmation. Other programs may read your clipboard, so clear it after use. Offline caches hold previously synced content and are not a substitute for separate backups.
